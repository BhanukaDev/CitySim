A Grid That Follows the Road

Zoning on a cell grid, as in Cities: Skylines, but the grid isn't square: each road side gets a strip of cells with columns square to the road and rows parallel to it, so it bends with curves and meets other roads cleanly at corners. You paint cells; the card's form groups them into plots; the style picks the buildings. Hover a cell to read it. This replaces the free-parcel storyboard (kept in zoning-storyboard.html).
