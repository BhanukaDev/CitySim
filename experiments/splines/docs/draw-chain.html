A click builds the road up to that point. While you move to the next point, only the new leg is a preview. What's built stays built and solid, and your own road counts as a road you can cross.

What changes

  1. Preview. The built road stays drawn as built while you move. The ghost starts where the corner at your last point begins and runs to the cursor.
  2. The corner at the last point. Its rounded arc is part of the preview, because Shift+wheel still sizes it until you click. After the click it's built like the rest.
  3. Crossing your own road makes a junction, the same as crossing any other road. So loops and figure eights work. This also applies to a single leg that crosses an earlier part of the same road.
  4. Unchanged: each click builds its leg, double-click or Enter ends the chain, RMB or Esc stops, Ctrl+Z takes back a leg, and a click on a dead end finishes.
