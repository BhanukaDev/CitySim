Kerb handles

A selected junction gets two kinds of control. A knob on each kerb sets that corner's size and keeps it a round arc. A road handle on each road moves the kerbs on both sides of that road together, and flares them up the road when its neighbours stay small.

What I heard

  • Kerbs are too big. They used the road's bend radius (16 m street, 60 m avenue). They now have their own, smaller KerbRadius per profile. Built.
  • Road handles stay. One per road, on its centre line where its kerbs start. Drag it along the road: both kerbs beside that road get bigger or tighter at that road's end. Built.
  • Add a control per kerb. Each corner can also be sized on its own. It sits on the kerb itself, like the radius knob on a road bend, not as more handles along the road, which would crowd a 12 m street. New in this version.
  • Same limits. Min and max kerb radius from the profile (the min holds unless Anarchy is on), a kerb's far end at most 4 × its near end, and nothing past what fits on the road.

Try it

Edit tool, junction selected. Drag a knob (dark ring on a kerb) in or out across its corner, or a road handle (white disc on a centre line) along its road. Focus one and use the arrow keys, or double-click it to reset. Pale tracks show how far each can go.

How it behaves in the Edit tool

  • Knobs and road handles show on the selected junction only. They highlight on hover; hovering never changes anything.
  • Knob: drag across the corner (along the line from the corner point through the kerb's middle). The kerb is a round arc of that radius, both ends moving. Tag kerb R 6 → 10 m.
  • Road handle: drag along the road. Both kerbs beside it change at this road's end, scaled together so a difference set with their knobs is kept. Tag kerbs R 6 · 9 → 8 · 12 m; a lopsided kerb shows both ends, R 6–14.
  • Limits for both: each kerb end within the profile's min and max (the min unless Anarchy), a far end at most 4 × the near end, and no kerb starting past what fits on its road. At a limit the tag turns amber and says which: most that fits, max R 16 m, max flare, tightest.
  • 0.5 m radius steps. Each release is one undo step. Double-click a knob or handle to reset it; the junction's radial menu has Reset kerbs for all of them.
  • Data: each road end stores two radii, one for the kerb on each side (null = the profile's). They stay with the road through moves, splits, merges and reconnects; one that no longer fits is squeezed to fit.

Questions for you

  1. Road handle after knob edits. When a road's two kerbs differ (say 6 and 12 from their knobs), should its handle scale both and keep the difference, or snap both to the same radius? I'd keep the difference (the live frame does that), so knob tweaks aren't lost by a later handle drag.
  2. Knob on a lopsided kerb. Grabbing it makes the kerb round again at the radius under the cursor. I think that's what a player expects; the alternative is scaling both ends and keeping the flare.
